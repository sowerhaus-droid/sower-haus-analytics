Private social-media performance reporting for Sower Haus.

About the Application

Sower Haus Analytics is being developed to review performance of our authorized TikTok account, including account statistics and video engagement metrics.

This application is intended for internal reporting and decision-making, not automated publishing.

Access requires authorization from the TikTok account holder. Available features depend on TikTok permissions.
