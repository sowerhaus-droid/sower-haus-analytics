Sower Haus Analytics

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Overview

Sower Haus Analytics is an internal reporting application being developed for Sower Haus. This policy describes our intended handling of information accessed through TikTok.

Information We Access

With authorization from the TikTok account holder, the application may access account identifiers, public profile information, follower statistics, and video engagement metrics such as views, likes, comments counts, and shares.

How We Use Information

Information is used to evaluate social media performance, prepare internal reports, and improve Sower Haus marketing decisions. We do not sell analytics data to advertisers.

Data Storage and Security

Authorized Sower Haus operators and approved service tools may process analytics data. Access tokens should be stored using secure credential management, never in publicly accessible source files.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary for internal reporting and should be deleted when no longer required.

TikTok authorization may be revoked through the account's connected-app settings. Account holders may also request deletion by contacting us.

Contact Information

sowerhaus@gmail.com

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as application functionality and data practices change.
